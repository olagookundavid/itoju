Delete Your Account & Data

Itoju Health

You can permanently delete your Itoju account and all associated health data at any time, using either of the two methods below.

Option 1: Delete in the app

Open the Itoju app → Settings → Delete Account, then confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all health data stored on our servers. This action cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email myitojuhealth@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Delete my account".

We will verify the request and permanently delete your account and data within 30 days, and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, alias, date of birth, sign-in credentials)
  • All health data you've logged: cycle/symptom data, conditions, sleep, exercise, body measurements, and any other entries tied to your account

Once deletion is complete, this data cannot be recovered. Any copy of the app on your device that still has local data can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

See our Privacy Policy for more detail on how your data is handled. Questions: myitojuhealth@gmail.com
