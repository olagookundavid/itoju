Itoju Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026

Itoju Health ("Itoju", "we", "us") provides the Itoju mobile app, a personal health-tracking tool. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how to control or delete it.

Using Itoju without an account

Itoju can be used without creating an account. In this mode, everything you enter (symptoms, cycle data, sleep, exercise, and other health metrics) stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.

Information we collect if you create an account

Creating an account lets your data sync across devices and be backed up. When you do, we collect:

  • Account information: email address, an alias/display name, and date of birth.
  • Health information you choose to log: menstrual cycle and symptom data, medical conditions, sleep, exercise, body measurements, and similar entries you make in the app.
  • Sign-in information: if you sign in with Google, we receive a verified identity token from Google to confirm who you are — we do not receive your Google password.

Your device's biometric unlock (fingerprint/face) used for the in-app lock screen is handled entirely by your device's operating system. Itoju never receives or stores biometric data — it only receives a yes/no "unlocked" result from the OS.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and keep your data in sync across your devices.
  • To show you your own health data back (trends, summaries, reminders) inside the app.
  • To send account-related emails (e.g. password reset codes).

We do not use your health data for advertising, and Itoju does not contain any advertising or third-party analytics/tracking software.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your data. We use a small number of service providers strictly to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — to verify Google sign-in.
  • Resend — to deliver transactional emails (such as password-reset codes).
  • Koyeb — our infrastructure provider, which hosts our server and database.

These providers process data only on our behalf and only as needed to provide the service above.

Data security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored using industry-standard one-way hashing (bcrypt) — we cannot see or recover your password. Access to your health data requires an authenticated session tied to your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: see our dedicated account & data deletion page for step-by-step instructions, including how to request deletion without the app installed.
  • Access or correct your data: your data is visible and editable directly within the app at any time.
  • Use the app without an account at any time to avoid syncing data off your device.

Data retention

We retain account and health data for as long as your account is active, or until you request deletion as described above.

Children's privacy

Itoju is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your country).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
